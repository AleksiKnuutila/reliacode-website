---
title: "Why not just point ChatGPT at our invoices?"
question: "Why not just point ChatGPT at our invoices?"
sort: 50
---
Because "probably right" isn't good enough when the output is a dollar claim against a
supplier. An LLM can misread a tier threshold or invent a missing rate and sound equally
confident either way. We use deterministic, auditable arithmetic for every number we
report — LLMs help us read contracts faster, but they never get the final say on a dollar
figure.
